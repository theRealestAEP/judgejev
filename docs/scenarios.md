# Scenario catalog

100 authored setups, each with two evidence variants and seeded facts. The original 24 patterns are followed by 76 setups grouped into 19 reusable rule families. Each rule supports several distinct stories through compatible facts and evidence.

Each setup has a fixed, coherent subject and allegation. Its rule generates compatible measurements, identities, permissions, or timestamps. Five exhibits expose the relevant facts. Private expected outcomes support development tests; Jev receives the public accusation, exhibits, and player response.

Rounds exclude the last 12 setups and the rule families used in the last three rounds. The finite catalog supports continuous play with varied facts.
